# Commercial discovery — 14 September 2026

## Decision

Build a focused Swedish photo-to-report app for solo service businesses. Initial niche to validate: small cleaning businesses doing one-off or end-of-job work. The deliverable is a customer handover report, not a full field service system.

This is a product hypothesis, not validated revenue. No customer interviews, purchases, conversion metrics or expressed willingness to pay have been collected. No invented testimonials or market-size numbers.

## Candidates considered

| Candidate | Advantages | Reason to deprioritize |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generic quoting/invoicing | Obvious business workflow | Crowded; accounting, taxes, invoice numbering and integrations complicate a reliable small standalone product. |
| Generic AI writing tool | Easy prototype | Undifferentiated, ongoing inference costs, API/payment dependencies. |
| Consumer productivity app | Can work offline | Harder to identify a costly problem and acquire paying users affordably. |
| Focused customer handover reports | Real output, demonstrable in a minute, zero backend operating requirements | Free substitutes, local-data-loss risk, weak defensibility; selected only as a testable wedge. |

This is a qualitative screening, not a scored market study. Only the reporting/cleaning category received live source checks in this first pass.

## Commercial hypothesis to test

“For a small cleaning business, a consistent Swedish handover report with the right checklist and company presentation is worth paying for if it saves repeated after-job administration.”

Initial test prices (proposals, not observed market facts): 490 SEK one-time for a packaged edition; 1,900 SEK for company adaptation and onboarding. Clarify tax treatment and terms before selling. Do not build a fake client-side payment gate: a static app cannot securely enforce entitlements by hiding a button.

Since the public beta already includes the core workflow, paid value must be concrete: custom report design, reusable customer-specific checklists, installation support or a supported packaged release. If prospects value only the free core, stop the paid-product hypothesis or choose a different niche.

## Validation gate

Ask 10 relevant business owners to complete one report using their usual workflow and then Klart. Record time, completion without help, requested corrections, repeat intent and willingness to buy at an actual stated price. Passing internal target: at least 5 complete a real task, 3 want a second use and 2 agree to a clearly specified paid pilot. These are experiment criteria, not evidence already obtained.

No outreach is authorized beyond drafting; the recurring task must not contact owners itself. Prepare interview materials and let the user authorize actual contacts. If no external feedback arrives, continue technical quality work without calling demand validated.
